A quiet mystery about a night bus driver, a brass key, and an empty seat no one else remembers.

## The text

The night bus driver keeps a brass key round his neck. Every night at the last stop, he taps it twice on the empty seat and listens for the soft click. He has told no one whose seat it was, and wonders whether to leave the key on its hook tonight.
